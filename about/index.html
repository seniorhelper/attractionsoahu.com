About Attractions Oahu

Updated November 10, 2026

Attractions Oahu is a free guide to sightseeing, beaches, hikes and things to do on Oʻahu. It's written for visitors who want practical answers: which places need a reservation, what they cost, when they're closed and how to fit them into a trip.

Who runs this site

Attractions Oahu is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small independent web publishing and digital marketing company in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. Eye To Ad Media publishes several Hawaiʻi travel guides, including Best Hotels Hawaii and Best Diving Hawaii.

We aren't a tour company and we don't sell tickets. We can't book, change or refund anything for you. Reservations for places like Hanauma Bay, Diamond Head and the USS Arizona Memorial are made directly with the agencies that run them, and we link to those official sites.

How we research

  • Official sources first. Hours, fees and reservation rules come from the agency that runs the place: the City and County of Honolulu, Hawaiʻi State Parks (DLNR), the National Park Service and Recreation.gov. Our planning guides list their sources and the date we checked them.
  • No invented details. We don't make up prices, ratings, reviews or visitor numbers. When we can't confirm something, we say so or leave it out.
  • We update. Hawaiʻi changes its visitor rules more often than most places. When a fee or booking rule changes, we update the page and the date at the top.

Conditions in the ocean and on trails change daily. Use this site to plan, and follow lifeguards, rangers and posted signs on the day.

How the site is funded

The guide is free to read. It's supported by advertising, including ads from Google AdSense, and by featured placements for Hawaiʻi businesses. Paid placements are labeled "Featured" or "Sponsored" and their links are marked rel="sponsored". Advertisers don't get a say in our planning guides, and nobody can pay to change what we say about a public park or attraction. Read the privacy policy for how ad cookies work.

Spotted something out of date?

Tell us on the contact page. Include the page, what's changed and a link to the official source, and we'll check it and fix it.
